# IPv6 Support

Generated on 6 Jan 2026

[IP addresses](https://www.digitalocean.com/community/tutorials/understanding-ip-addresses-subnets-and-cidr-notation-for-networking) let machines communicate across a network. DigitalOcean Droplets are assigned IPv4 addresses by default. Enabling IPv6 on a Droplet gives you access to its 16 additional IPv6 addresses.

[](/support/how-do-i-disable-ipv6/)

![](https://docs.digitalocean.com/images/icons/support.svg)

How do I disable IPv6?

You can disable IPv6 by modifying your Droplet’s network interface configuration.

[](/support/are-my-droplets-ip-addresses-static/)

![](https://docs.digitalocean.com/images/icons/support.svg)

Are my Droplet's IP addresses static?

Addresses assigned to a Droplet remain static for the life of the Droplet.
