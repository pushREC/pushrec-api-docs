## What Counts as a Record?

Each "record" is one data item returned:

| Scraper Type | 1 Record = |
|--------------|------------|
| Video by URL | 1 video's metadata |
| Video search | 1 video in results |
| Channel profile | 1 channel's data |
| Comments | 1 comment |
