# App Platform Maintenance

Validated on 22 Nov 2024 • Last edited on 22 Nov 2024

App Platform is a fully managed Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) that deploys applications from Git repositories or container images. It automatically builds, deploys, and scales components while handling all underlying infrastructure.

As part of our commitment to maintaining a reliable platform, we regularly perform maintenance on App Platform to ensure its security, stability, and performance. During this maintenance, your application may be automatically redeployed. You should not experience any downtime during this process.

If the redeployment fails during maintenance, we notify you via your account’s email address. Once notified, you have a one-week grace period to resolve the issue. If the problem is not addressed within the grace period, the deployment is deleted and the app remains offline.
