# Slack Support - Trigger.dev

Based on the documentation page, here's what you need to know about accessing Slack support for Trigger.dev:

## How to Request Slack Support

Slack support is available exclusively to users on the **Trigger.dev Pro plan**. To request a private Slack Connect channel:

1. **Log into** the [Trigger.dev web app](https://cloud.trigger.dev)
2. **Ensure you have** an active paid plan subscription
3. **Locate the button** labeled "Join our Slack" in the bottom-left corner and click it

## What This Provides

A private Slack Connect channel enables direct communication with the Trigger.dev team for premium support access.

## Alternative Support Options

If you're not on a Pro plan, Trigger.dev offers other ways to get help:
- **Discord Community** - Join the public community channel
- **Email Support** - Contact the team directly through their website

This tiered support approach ensures that premium customers receive dedicated assistance while maintaining community resources for all users.
