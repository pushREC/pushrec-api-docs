 YouTube API Services - Branding Guidelines

## Overview
YouTube provides branding assets for developers integrating YouTube functionality. "YouTube APIs let you incorporate YouTube functionality into your application or device," with specific logos and usage rules required.

## Three Logo Types

**1. "Developed with YouTube" Logo**
Use when applications are "entirely dependent on curating YouTube content or on its integration with YouTube." Removing YouTube would make the app nonfunctional.

**2. YouTube Logo**
Use for "a specific application feature or component that uses YouTube content." The app remains useful without YouTube integration.

**3. YouTube Icon**
Reserved for attribution in multi-source environments with "space constraints" making larger logos impractical.

## Critical Requirements

**Naming Restrictions:**
- "Never use the YouTube name or any abbreviation, acronym, or variant" like YT or You-Tube in your app's name
- Cannot use YouTube branding with app names/descriptions

**Logo Usage Rules:**
- Must link to YouTube content or YouTube components
- Cannot alter, obscure, or distort imagery
- Cannot be the most prominent page element
- Maintain sufficient contrast with backgrounds
- Keep fully visible and unobstructed

**Prohibited Contexts:**
- Adult content websites
- Gambling or violence promotion
- Hate speech platforms
- Tobacco/alcohol sales to minors
- Any YouTube Terms of Service violations
