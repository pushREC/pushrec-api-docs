# OpenRouter Principles

OpenRouter is a platform helping developers optimize AI model usage across multiple providers. The service centers on a "multi-model and multi-provider" future.

## Key Offering Areas

### Economics & Performance

The platform identifies competitive pricing and optimized latencies across numerous providers, allowing developers to customize their prioritization strategy.

### API Standardization

Developers can switch between different models without code modifications. Users can even manage their own model selection and payment through the platform's authorization features.

### Market Intelligence

Early access to newly released models is available, along with usage analytics showing how different models perform across use cases. Community updates are shared via their Discord community.

### Streamlined Finances

A unified billing system simplifies payment regardless of how many providers are integrated.

### System Reliability

Automatic failover mechanisms and intelligent request routing maintain service continuity when providers experience outages.

### Improved Quotas

Direct partnerships with model providers yield higher request limits and greater throughput capacity.
