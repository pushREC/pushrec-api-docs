# Data Collection

OpenRouter maintains a straightforward approach to user data handling.

## Key Points

### Prompt Storage

OpenRouter does not store your prompts or responses, *unless* you have explicitly opted in to prompt logging in your account settings.

### Sampling for Categorization

The platform samples select prompts for categorization purposes to support reporting and model rankings. If you haven't enabled prompt logging, this categorization remains completely anonymous and disconnected from your identity or user account.

### Metadata Retention

OpenRouter does retain request metadata including:
- Token counts
- Latency information

This data powers their reporting features and your activity feed, but does not include the content of your prompts or responses.
